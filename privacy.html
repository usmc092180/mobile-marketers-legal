Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

1. Overview

Mobile Marketers operates an internal tool that connects to TikTok accounts we own. This policy explains what data the tool handles.

2. Data we access

With authorization from the account owner, the tool reads public profile information, video performance statistics, and comments on our own accounts' videos.

3. How we use it

We use this data only to understand how our content performs and to respond to our audience. We do not sell, rent, or share this data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Storage and security

Data is stored on private systems controlled by Mobile Marketers. Access tokens are kept in protected storage and are never published.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep data only as long as it is useful for analytics and moderation. You can request deletion of any data about you, and you can revoke the tool's access at any time from your TikTok account settings.
